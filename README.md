## 📌 Problem Statement

Under the Government of India's **"Shree Anna" (millets) initiative**, farmers, self-help groups (SHGs), and farmer producer organizations (FPOs) lack a unified digital platform to trade, track, and add value to millet produce — leading to poor price realization, opaque supply chains, and low consumer awareness of millets' nutritional benefits.

---

## 💡 Solution Overview

**MilletChain** is a comprehensive digital platform connecting **farmers, SHGs, FPOs, processors, startups, and consumers** in one ecosystem.

- 🔗 **Blockchain-backed traceability** — every transaction across the millet value chain is transparent and secure
- 🤖 **AI-powered modules** — real-time pricing, quality certification via image recognition, and demand forecasting
- 🚚 **Integrated logistics & payments** — digital payment gateways aligned with government subsidy schemes
- 🛒 **E-commerce support** — for value-added millet products, plus educational content on nutrition
- 🌐 **Multilingual & offline access** — SMS/USSD support for low-connectivity rural areas

### Core Features
🌾 Blockchain &nbsp;·&nbsp; 🤖 AI Analytics &nbsp;·&nbsp; 🚚 Logistics &nbsp;·&nbsp; 💰 Subsidy Integration &nbsp;·&nbsp; 📱 Offline Access

---

## 🏗️ How It Works

1. **Farmer Registration** — farmer provides personal and farm details
2. **Produce Listing** — farmer lists produce with quality data
3. **Buyer Browsing** — buyers explore available produce options
4. **Purchase** — buyer selects and buys produce
5. **Shipment Tracking** — blockchain tracks the produce shipment
6. **Payment Processing** — payment is processed with subsidy integration

## 📊 Feasibility & Viability

| Aspect | Details |
|---|---|
| Development Cost | $75,000 – $150,000 |
| Scalability | AWS cloud infrastructure |
| Pilot States | Karnataka, Rajasthan |
| Target Users | 50,000+ in first year |

**Key challenges & strategies:**
- Low digital literacy → digital literacy workshops + voice-based UI
- Data privacy/security → end-to-end encryption + PDP Bill compliance
- Rural logistics gaps → AI-driven logistics planning, partnership with India Post
- Regulatory compliance → collaboration with Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare (MoA&FW)

## 🌍 Impact

- Empowers **10 million+ smallholder farmers and SHGs** with direct market access
- Enables farmers to earn **20–30% higher prices** by cutting out intermediaries
- Promotes climate-resilient, millet-based agriculture and reduced water use
- Builds SHG communities and promotes women-led FPOs
- Improves nutritional security and millet adoption among consumers
